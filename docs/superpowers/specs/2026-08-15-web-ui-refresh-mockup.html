Neapolitan Pizza

1×
6 servings 20 min Italian Advanced stadlermade.com italianpizza

Ingredients

6
  • Pizza Dough6 balls
  • San Marzano tomato sauce5 tbsp
  • mozzarella cheese100 g
  • basil leaves
  • flour
  • semolina

Cookware

2
  • outdoor oven
  • spatula

Method

6 steps
  1. 1

    Preheat your outdoor oven so it's around 450/500°C (842/932°F).

  2. 2

    Prepare your pizza toppings because from now on you wanna work fast. Sprinkle some semolina on your work surface.

    semolina
  3. 3

    Use a spatula to remove the Pizza Dough from the dough box and place it upside down in the flour. Sprinkle the bottom of the dough ball generously with semolina, then flip it over again so that the top remains the top.

    Pizza Dough 6 ballsfloursemolina
  4. 4

    Press with your fingertips from both hands in the center of the dough ball towards the outer edges so that the air goes to the crust. Rotate the dough clockwise and make the same movement again until you have gone all around and a nice crust has formed.

  5. 5

    Place the base on a lightly floured surface and spread San Marzano tomato sauce on it. Add some fresh basil leaves and fresh mozzarella cheese. Bake for ⏱ 90 sec.

    San Marzano tomato sauce 5 tbspbasil leavesmozzarella cheese 100 g
